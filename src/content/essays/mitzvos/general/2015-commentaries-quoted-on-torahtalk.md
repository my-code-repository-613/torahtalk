*In the past, whenever I quoted a commentary in one of my TorahTalks, I provided a link with information about that author.  However, I have found that over time, many of these links have gone dead.*

*Naturally, it is impossible for me to constantly update every link on every post I have ever written.  Therefore, I will now be sending all links to this page, which I will hopefully be able to keep current.  (It will also allow me to provide more than one link to a given personality.)*

*Over the next several months I will be adding more links to this page.  Please contact me at [TorahTalk@gmail.com](mailto:TorahTalk@gmail.com) let me know if you come across a link that needs updating.*
