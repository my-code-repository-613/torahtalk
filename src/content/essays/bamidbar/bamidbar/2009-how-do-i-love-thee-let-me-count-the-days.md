SHAVUOS Next Thursday night is the beginning of the Holiday of Shavuos.  Shavuos commemorates the day, 50 days after the Exodus from Egypt, when G-d gave us the Torah. For more on Shavuos click  [here](http://www.ou.org/chagim/shavuot/). **"How do I Love Thee?  Let me Count the DAYS”**  G-d seems to take great interest in knowing how many Israelites there are.  He can’t seem to stop counting us!  He commissioned a census after the Exodus from Egypt (Exodus, 12:37), after the sin of the Golden Calf (ibid. 38:26), and now again, a little over a year after the Exodus. Why does he keep counting us?  Doesn’t He know how many we are?!  [Rashi](http://www.ou.org/about/judaism/rashi.htm) explains that the repeated counts demonstrate His love for the Jewish People. [R](http://www.ou.org/about/judaism/rabbis/levovitz.htm)abbi Yerucham Levovitz explained Rashi’s comments.  Picture a wealthy man sitting in his treasure house counting his money.  How many times does he need to count?  He already knows, from the first count, how much he has.  Why does he continue? Because he loves his money!!  Every gold and silver coin jingles as it drops back into the money bag.  He is so caught up with his love of money that he just sits there counting it, again and again and again. That’s how much G-d loves you.  He adores you, His precious and beloved child!  Therefore He counts us, again and again and again. We, too, have been counting: *You will count for yourselves, from the day after the rest day(i.e, from the second day of Passover)… seven complete weeks…*  (Exodus, 23:15) We are counting the days from Passover, the celebration of our release from Egyptian bondage, until Shavuos, the celebration of our standing at Mount Sinai to receive the Torah;  the REASON for our release from Egyptian bondage. G-d took his precious children out of Egypt on Passover so that He could give us His precious gift of the Torah seven weeks later.  We are supposed to count, with eager anticipation, looking forward to receiving G-d’s precious gift. Every Passover, we relive the Exodus.  This  Friday, is Day #43 in our 49-day count to that special day when we will re-live that wonderful experience of receiving the Torah. Do we love G-d as much as He loves us?  Do we love His Torah as much as He expects us to love His Torah?  Do we count the days with eagerness to grab every opportunity to fulfill His Mitzvahs? Every day is a gift.  We should count these valuable gifts, appreciating how dear and rare they are.  A rich man counts his money because he loves his money.  G-d counts us because He loves us.  We need to count the days we have, filling each one with holiness and meaning. The dividends it will pay will be countless! Have a great Shabbos and Yom Tov.
