Adam and Eve were invited into the Garden of Eden; a literal Paradise where all their needs were met.  There was just one catch.  There was this tree…

The snake convinced Eve, and Eve convinced Adam.

G-d asked Adam if he had eaten from the forbidden Tree of Knowledge.  It’s an interesting question.

G-d certainly knew the answer; Adam had given in to temptation.  He had sinned.  The question now was, would he repent?  Would he choose to do the right thing and confess?  Would he choose to do the right thing and ask for forgiveness?

What he did was throw his wife (and G-d!!) under the bus!
